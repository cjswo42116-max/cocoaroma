Before you ask

FAQ

Do you speak English?

Enough to take good care of you. Our menu and consultation are in English, and we’re used to welcoming travelers. If anything’s unclear, WhatsApp us anytime.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking a day or two ahead — evenings fill up first. For same-day, message us; if there’s an open room, you’re welcome.

Can we come together?

Yes — we have private single rooms, and rooms for two or three when you come together. Tell us your group size when you book.

What should I wear or bring?

Nothing special. Gowns and everything you need are prepared. Just arrive 10 minutes early for your foot bath.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Mastercard, Visa and all major cards, or cash — in Korean won. 10% VAT is added for card payments.

Should I tip?

No — tipping isn’t customary in Korea. A kind review means more to us.

Is the oil scented?

Jeju Aroma Therapy uses a water-soluble, unscented oil, so nothing distracts from the work of the therapist’s hands. Any remaining oil is wiped away cleanly to finish.

I’m pregnant / I have a condition. Can I still come?

Please tell us when you book so we can prepare the right care for you — and consult your therapist for treatment specifics.

Can I bring my kids?

Children are welcome with a parent — Kids’ Growth Therapy (60 min) is on our menu.
